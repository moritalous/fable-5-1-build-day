At Build Day you will run Claude Code on **API credits**. That requires an account on [Claude Platform](https://platform.claude.com), the developer console (the site itself is labelled “Claude Console”). **Please create one before the event.** It takes about 3 minutes.

<Aside type="caution" title="This is not the same as your claude.ai account">
	This is a **separate account** from your subscription account on the [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) chat service (including Pro/Max plans). Create an account on the developer-facing **Claude Platform**.

	Even if you already subscribe to Claude Pro/Max, you need a Claude Platform account to receive credits. (You can still use Claude Code on Pro/Max as is — see [First run](/en/preparation/first-run/) for details.)
</Aside>

## Create an account

<Steps>

1. Go to [platform.claude.com](https://platform.claude.com) and create an account. You can sign up with a Google account or an email address.

    ![](../../preparation/images/2026-07-04-03-29-32.png)

1. Enter your name and other details.

    ![](../../preparation/images/2026-07-04-05-51-41.png)

1. You will be asked for payment information, but choose 「今はスキップ」 (Skip for now). Credits are handed out on the day of the event.

    ![](../../preparation/images/2026-07-04-05-51-50.png)

1. Your account is created.

    ![](../../preparation/images/2026-07-04-05-51-56.png)

1. You can change the display language here. Setting it to Japanese makes it easier to follow the explanations on the day.

    ![](../../preparation/images/2026-07-04-05-56-09.png)

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="You do not need to buy credits">
	We plan to hand out API credits to participants. There is no need to buy any in advance. See [Getting your API credits](/en/credits/) for how to receive them.
</Aside>
