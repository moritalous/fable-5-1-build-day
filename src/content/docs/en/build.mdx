Once the setup is done, all that's left is to build. **What you build is up to you.** We don't set a theme or a subject.

## The three tracks

There are three tracks for the kind of thing you might build. They describe **not which field you build in, but what quality of thing you build**.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Delight" icon="star">
		**Something surprising, beautiful, or fun.**

		The test is whether you can say "I couldn't have built something this good, this fast, before."
	</Card>
	<Card title="Breakthrough" icon="rocket">
		**Something you couldn't build before.**

		Bring a problem the previous model couldn't handle and see how far you get. The comparison itself is the result.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Everyday" icon="laptop">
		**Something that solves a problem around you.**

		Take something that actually bothers you and turn it into something you can actually use.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Solo or in a team

Form a team of 2–4 people on the spot, or build on your own — either is fine.

<Aside type="tip" title="For a quick start">
	The fastest way to start is to copy a prompt from the [Showcase](/en/showcase/), send it as it is, and steer the result in your own direction. You don't have to think one up from scratch.
</Aside>

## After you've built it

### Submit what you built

If you submit through Anthropic's official form, "Share what you built with Claude!", **your work becomes a candidate to be featured on Claude's social accounts and elsewhere**. You put the work in, so do send it.

The form takes about 7 minutes. It doesn't have to be on the day — feel free to submit it once you've settled down.
